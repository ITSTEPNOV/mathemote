# Related research

This note records three documented connections. It identifies differences in purpose and representation. A literature survey of this scope cannot establish global priority.

## Chernoff Faces

Herman Chernoff used properties of cartoon faces, such as the curvature of the mouth, to show separate dimensions of a multivariate data point. Stepnov's Method uses the mouth and eyebrows as two parts of the code for a single decimal order relative to a stated window. Mathemote also offers sequential and simultaneous forms of delivery. [Chernoff, *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 1973](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1973.10482434) · [Paper](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~rnugent/PCMI2016/papers/ChernoffFaces.pdf).

## Order of Magnitude Markers

Rita Borgo, Joel Dearden and Mark W. Jones proposed visual markers that present a number’s significand and exponent within one symbol. They compared marker variants with other representations in detection and comparison tasks. Stepnov's Method uses a facial code for an order and preserves the exact number separately. The paper's results apply to the representations and conditions it tested. [Borgo, Dearden and Jones, *IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics*, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2014.2346428) · [Author manuscript](https://vis.cs.ucdavis.edu/vis2014papers/TVCG/papers/2261_20tvcg12-borgo-2346428.pdf).

## Graphical perception

William S. Cleveland and Robert McGill studied elementary visual decoding tasks in quantitative graphs. Their framework motivates evaluating a proposed visual code by measuring how people read and compare values. Mathemote's [proposed pilot](readability-pilot.md) measures its own reading tasks and recognizes that the findings from other visual encodings do not validate its facial code. [Cleveland and McGill, *Journal of the American Statistical Association*, 1984](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1984.10478080).
